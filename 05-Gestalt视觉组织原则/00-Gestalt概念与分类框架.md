# 05 格式塔（Gestalt）视觉感知原则

课件原文审阅稿。依据《交互信息设计 演示文稿》第 6.2 节（第 34–42 页），按课件顺序收录 9 项。中文标题、英文标题和插图中的定义按页转录，合并了版面换行。

## 6.2.1 邻近原则 接近原则（第 34 页）

**Law of Proximity**

> Elements that are placed closer together are perceived as being more related than those spaced further apart.

## 6.2.2 简单原则（第 35 页）

**Law of Simplicity**

> People will perceive and interpret ambiguous or complex images as the simplest forms possible.

## 6.2.3 相似原则（第 36 页）

**Law of Similarity**

> Objects that look similar in shape, size or color are perceived as being grouped together or related.

## 6.2.4 闭合原则（第 37 页）

**Law of Closure**

> Our minds tend to fill in missing information and ignore gaps to create familiar shapes and images.

## 6.2.5 对称原则（第 38 页）

**Law of Symmetry**

> Objects that are balanced and symmetrical are seen as complete or whole.

## 6.2.6 连续性原则（第 39 页）

**Law of Continuity**

> We perceive lines as part of a continuous movement in order to minimize abrupt changes. In this composition, we perceive two overlapping wavy lines instead of three shapes linked together.

## 6.2.7 同步原则（第 40 页）

**Law of Synchrony**

> Elements that move in the same direction are perceived as more related than objects that move in different directions.
